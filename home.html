OUR STORY

A little piece of Lagos, right here in Nairobi

Kitchen began with a simple craving: the smoky party jollof, the peppered asun, the comfort of pounded yam and egusi. The food that means home.

Today we serve those flavors to Nairobi from two kitchens, cooking everything fresh and seasoned the way it should be. Whether you dine in, grab takeout, or order to your door, you get the real thing.
